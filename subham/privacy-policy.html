Subham — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HyperZeta ("we", "us") handles your information in the Subham Android app (package com.hyperzeta.subham, "the app"). Subham helps families plan weddings and related ceremonies — rituals, tasks, budgets, vendors, and moi (gift) records. The app is intended for users aged 13 and older.

Summary

  • Signing in is required, by phone OTP or Google, through Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Everything you plan in the app — events, rituals, tasks, budget, vendors, guests, and moi entries — is stored only on your device. This version has no cloud sync and no server of ours holds it.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • There are no ads. The app is free today; paid subscription features may be added later, billed through Google Play.
  • Crash reports and basic usage analytics are collected through Google Firebase so we can fix faults.
  • You can delete your account and wipe all app data from Profile → Account → Delete account.

We hold no copy of your planning data. It never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. The only information that reaches a server is your sign-in identity (held by Firebase Authentication) and the crash/analytics data described above.

Information about other people

Subham lets you record details of other people — guest and vendor contacts (name, phone, email, city, address, notes) and moi entries (a family name, place, occasion, and the gift given or received). You enter this yourself and it is stored only on your device. You are responsible for having a proper basis to record another person's details, and for handling any request they make about that record. Because we never receive this data, we cannot access, export, or delete it for them — you can, inside the app.

Permissions

The app uses INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for sign-in, crash reporting and analytics; POST_NOTIFICATIONS for task and ritual reminders (off until you allow it); SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM so reminders arrive on the minute rather than batched; RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to re-arm reminders after a restart; and USE_BIOMETRIC for the optional app lock. The app requests no storage, gallery, contacts or SMS permission: profile photos come through the Android photo picker and the sign-in code through the SMS Retriever API, neither of which needs one. The Firebase Analytics SDK also declares the advertising ID permission (AD_ID); you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Biometrics never reach the app. The app lock asks Android to confirm your fingerprint, face or screen lock and receives only a yes/no answer. No biometric data is read, stored or transmitted by Subham.

Third-party services

We use Google Firebase to operate the app: Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (usage and stability). Google Play services supports sign-in on the device. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. See also Firebase's privacy and security information. To verify a phone number, Firebase sends an SMS through its carrier partners and may use your phone number and device information for abuse prevention.

Paid subscriptions. Subham is free today and contains no in-app purchases. If paid features are introduced, they will be billed through Google Play Billing. In that case we would receive your purchase and subscription status so the app can unlock what you paid for — never your card number or bank details, which Google handles. This policy will be updated before any such feature ships.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Your planning data is never shared with anyone. Sign-in, crash and analytics data is shared only with Google as the provider of the services listed above, and is encrypted in transit.

Data retention and deletion

Your planning data lives only on your device, so you control it completely:

  • Delete individual items — any event, task, expense, vendor, contact or moi entry can be deleted inside the app.
  • Delete your account — Profile → Account → Delete account deletes your Firebase account and then wipes every table and preference on the device. This is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Sign out — Profile → Account → Sign out deliberately keeps your data on the device so you can sign back in and resume planning.
  • Uninstall — removes all app data from the device. Note that uninstalling alone does not delete your Firebase account; use Delete account for that, or email us.

Firebase Authentication keeps your account record until the account is deleted. Crashlytics retains crash reports for up to 90 days; analytics data is retained for the period set in our Google Analytics configuration. Since we hold no copy of your planning data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For any data request, contact us at the email below and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

App data is kept in Android's private per-app storage, which other apps cannot read. You can turn on App lock (Profile → Account) to require your screen lock or biometric before Subham opens. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but keeping your planning data off any server of ours removes most of the risk.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or the California CCPA — you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data relating to you, to withdraw consent, and to object to or restrict certain processing. We do not sell personal information. Your planning data is on-device and not held by us; for your account identity and crash/analytics data, use Delete account in the app, or contact us at the email below.

Children and teenagers

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it and the associated account.

If you are between 13 and the age of majority where you live, please use Subham with a parent or guardian's involvement — they should review this policy with you, and their consent is required for any paid subscription. Where local law sets a higher minimum age for consenting to data processing (for example 16 in parts of the EU), that age applies instead of 13.

Changes to this policy
